# Alert Specification

> Generated by spec-gen v1.0.0 on 2026-05-10 22:19

## Purpose

Manages stock alert notifications by detecting trigger conditions, preventing duplicate alerts, and
sending email notifications with consolidated summaries.

## Requirements

> `agents/alert/alert_agent.py`

### Requirement: ProcessStockAlerts

The system SHALL processes a batch of stock records to identify and send alerts for stocks meeting trigger conditions.

#### Scenario: ValidStockTriggersAlert
<!-- spec-gen-test: tags=smoke (auto) -->
- **GIVEN** Stock meets alert trigger conditions and hasn't been alerted recently
- **WHEN** processStockAlerts is called
- **THEN** Alert is sent and recorded in database

#### Scenario: RecentlyAlertedStockSkipped
- **GIVEN** Stock meets trigger conditions but was alerted within cooldown period
- **WHEN** processStockAlerts is called
- **THEN** No alert is sent and stock is skipped

### Requirement: CheckAlertTrigger

The system SHALL evaluates a stock record against alert criteria to determine if it warrants an immediate alert.

#### Scenario: StockMeetsTriggerCriteria
- **GIVEN** Stock has qualifying technical indicators or patterns
- **WHEN** checkAlertTrigger is called
- **THEN** Returns appropriate trigger label

#### Scenario: StockDoesNotMeetCriteria
- **GIVEN** Stock lacks qualifying conditions
- **WHEN** checkAlertTrigger is called
- **THEN** Returns None

### Requirement: ValidateAlertEligibility

The system SHALL determines if a stock should receive an alert based on trigger conditions and cooldown period.

#### Scenario: EligibleStockWithTrigger
- **GIVEN** Stock has alert trigger and is not in cooldown period
- **WHEN** validateAlertEligibility is called
- **THEN** Returns true

#### Scenario: StockInCooldownPeriod
- **GIVEN** Stock was recently alerted within cooldown hours
- **WHEN** validateAlertEligibility is called
- **THEN** Returns false

### Requirement: SendDailySummary

The system SHALL sends a consolidated daily summary email with portfolio positions and alerts.

#### Scenario: SummaryWithPositions
- **GIVEN** Portfolio positions are provided
- **WHEN** sendDailySummary is called
- **THEN** Email sent with position details and stop loss narratives

#### Scenario: SummaryWithoutPositions
- **GIVEN** No positions provided
- **WHEN** sendDailySummary is called
- **THEN** Email sent with general market summary
