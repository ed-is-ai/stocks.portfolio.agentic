patterns and generate screening reports.

#### Requirement: CalculateMatchRatio

The system SHALL determine what fraction of current holders were also present in previous quarter

#### Scenario: HighContinuity
- **GIVEN** 80 out of 100 current holders were also in previous quarter
- **WHEN** calculate_match_ratio is called
- **THEN** match ratio SHALL be 0.8
